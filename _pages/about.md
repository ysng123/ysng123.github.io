    <p class="hero-copy" data-zh="我是&lt;strong&gt;童昊阳&lt;/strong&gt;，本科毕业于中国科学院大学人工智能学院。我将于 2026 年 9 月进入中关村学院与中国科学院自动化研究所联合培养博士项目，导师为中国科学院自动化研究所 MAIS 与 NLPR 的赫然教授。">I am <strong>Haoyang Tong</strong>. I recently completed my bachelor's degree at the School of Artificial Intelligence, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. In September 2026, I will begin the joint PhD program between ZGCA and CASIA, advised by Prof. Ran He in MAIS &amp; NLPR, CASIA.</p>
    <p class="hero-copy" data-zh="我的研究方向包括生成模型、大语言模型和世界模型，目前重点关注像素空间与三维空间中的可控生成。">My research explores generative models, large language models, and world models, with a current focus on controllable generation across pixels and 3D space.</p>
